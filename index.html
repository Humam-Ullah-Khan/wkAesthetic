Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Are consultations required before treatment?

Yes. Every treatment begins with a consultation so we can understand your goals, assess your skin, and recommend the most suitable options.

Are treatments safe?

All treatments are performed by trained professionals using approved products and technology. Safety and client wellbeing are our highest priorities.

How long do results last?

This depends on the treatment. Some results last several months, while others may last longer. Your practitioner will explain what to expect during your consultation.

Is there any downtime?

Many treatments require little to no downtime, allowing you to return to normal activities quickly.
